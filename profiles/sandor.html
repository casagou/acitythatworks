Who they are

Jack Sandor is a 27-year-old Red Seal electrician, housing and transit advocate and lifelong Victoria-area resident. He grew up on Warren Gardens in Gonzales, attended Margaret Jenkins, Central and Oak Bay schools, and now rents near Stadacona Park with his fiancée. His construction work has ranged from deeply subsidized seniors’ housing to townhomes, condominiums, luxury homes and a hospital. He has served as vice-president of Homes for Living and on the BetterTransitYYJ board and volunteers at the Fernwood NRG Community Dinner. For voters, his strongest distinction is a blue-collar construction perspective paired with one of the field’s most detailed newcomer platforms; his advocacy affiliations should be weighed openly when assessing housing and transportation proposals.
