Profile. Wendy Bowkett is a business strategist, community volunteer and former long-serving Victoria Downtown Residents’ Association leader. She lived downtown for more than 20 years and served on the association’s board for over a decade in executive roles, including work on governance, resident engagement and relationships among neighbourhood, business and civic stakeholders. She also serves on the board of The Soup Kitchen and holds an MBA. For voters, Bowkett brings direct downtown and organizational-governance experience; the central test is whether her broad “back to basics” approach includes measurable funding choices and service commitments.

2026 campaign and social verification. wendybowkett.ca is a current, authorized campaign site with donations, events, updates, volunteering and sign requests. Its programme now has three detailed pillars: restore public trust through ongoing engagement, reporting, evaluation and responsible use of tax dollars; improve public safety by properly resourcing the system, supporting appropriate non-police crisis teams and activating public spaces; and make Victoria more liveable and affordable by accelerating non-market and co-operative housing approvals, restoring Local Area Plans, expanding the tree canopy and creating a strategic economic plan. X @wdbowkett is the clearly attributable active campaign channel located. The website does not link other social profiles, and no current dedicated Facebook, Instagram, Bluesky, Threads, TikTok or YouTube campaign account was verified.
