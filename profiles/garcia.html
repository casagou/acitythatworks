Background

Evidence vintage (summary). Verified 1 Sep 2026 against the complete current campaign site, biography, five-outcome platform, July launch release, 31 Aug election coverage and campaign-linked Facebook and Instagram accounts. The former stub is obsolete.

Web, campaign & socials (checked 1 Sep 2026). Campaign site · Facebook · Instagram · no campaign X, Bluesky, Threads, TikTok or YouTube account was linked

Profile. Jerry Garcia is a retired professional engineer, executive, entrepreneur and community volunteer with more than four decades of experience leading organizations and complex projects. His campaign biography describes work in Toronto, Montreal, Tokyo, Miami and elsewhere with global companies, governments, health-care organizations and small businesses, as well as teaching entrepreneurs. Garcia and his spouse chose downtown Victoria as their home eight years ago. For voters, his strongest case is engineering and executive delivery experience; the key scrutiny points are whether his ambitious performance targets use verifiable baselines and whether City and CRD authorities can deliver them.

2026 campaign and social verification. jerryforvictoria.ca is a current campaign site for both Victoria City Council and CRD director, with a biography, articles, volunteer and donation functions and five “transformational changes”: grow the economy; restore safe public spaces; end chronic homelessness through housing, treatment and recovery; build complete neighbourhoods; and deliver faster, more transparent government. It commits to cutting permit times by at least 50%, reducing homelessness by 85%, publishing performance dashboards, using evidence-based budgeting, improving lighting and cleaning, supporting visible patrols and community safety teams, coordinating treatment and supportive housing, attracting investment and using smart-city technology. The site labels itself “Jerry 1.0” and says a second version is coming, so details may still change. Facebook and Instagram are linked directly and are the verified campaign channels; no other official social network was linked.

Sources. Campaign site, 19 Jul press-release page, 30 Jul launch material, 31 Aug CTV election coverage and campaign-linked social accounts. Career details and the 85% homelessness target are campaign-attributed pending independent baseline verification.

Current evidence requiring first full score. Direct commitments now overlap with M7, M11, M14, M26/M27, M30, M53, M65, M67, M70b/M70c, M71 and M80. The hard permit and homelessness targets are unusual in this field, but the campaign has not yet published baseline years, responsible agencies, annual milestones, funding, treatment capacity, staffing or a definition of “homelessness reduced.” No position was located on the tax glide path, debt and reserve rules, managed competition, major-capital referendums, tenant protection, climate adaptation, host-Nations implementation or municipal AI governance.
