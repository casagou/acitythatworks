Profile. Peter Rose Gibbs is a parent, lifelong Victorian and community organizer with nearly two decades of experience pressing governments and institutions for change. His current professional profile identifies him as an organizing manager, and his campaign launch emphasizes relationship-building and collective action rather than prior elected experience. For voters, Gibbs offers a labour- and movement-organizing perspective and is running as part of Victoria For All, an openly working-class, democratic-socialist electoral organization; that shared slate identity is central to understanding both his policy programme and likely council alliances.

2026 campaign and social verification. Victoria For All formally launched Gibbs for City Council on 15 Aug alongside incumbent Susan Kim and school-board candidate Maiya Beech. Its four shared pillars are Housing for All, including doubled City spending on public, non-profit and co-operative housing and protection of affordable rentals; Care for All, including a 24-hour mental-health crisis response and expanded outreach and harm reduction; Safe Climate for All, including expanded free transit, heat pumps, green roofs and urban trees; and Community for All, including gathering places and arts, recreation and cultural events. Gibbs’s Instagram is current and explicitly identifies him as a parent and council candidate with Victoria For All. The slate’s Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Threads and Bluesky accounts are linked from its site. No personal Facebook, X, YouTube, Bluesky, Threads or TikTok account was independently verified.

Sources. Victoria For All, candidate/slate page, Times Colonist launch coverage, CHEK launch coverage, Victoria News coverage, personal Instagram and public professional profile.

Current evidence requiring first full score. The slate programme creates direct evidence around M6/M6b, M9, M11, M20b/M23b, M26b, M50, M58, M64 and M80. Missing are candidate-specific priorities, costs, housing and service baselines, transit jurisdiction, staffing, CRD funding, implementation dates, fiscal methods, permit standards, a performance dashboard and governance commitments. Shared slate promises should be scored as Gibbs’s current programme, while clearly identified as organizational rather than individually authored.
