Profile. Marg Gardiner is a first-term Victoria councillor with a background in chemistry, public management, regulatory enforcement, programme management and data analysis. She holds a BSc in chemistry and an MBA in public management/administration, has lived in Victoria for more than two decades and served for 16 years on the James Bay Neighbourhood Association board before election. Her community work has covered public safety, land use, accessibility, transportation, parks, economic development and air quality. On council she has been one of the most consistent fiscal- and public-safety dissenters, opposing each annual tax increase and the Missing Middle policy. For voters, her campaign offers the clearest method-based alternative on budgeting and neighbourhood consultation, while her record on policing, supervised consumption and land use makes the substantive trade-offs unusually explicit.

2026 campaign and social verification. marggardiner.ca is now a current, authorized campaign site for City Council and CRD director with biography, priorities, voting record, endorsements, donations, volunteering, news, voter survey and campaign email sign-up. It is organized around four priorities: safe streets, liveable neighbourhoods, respect for the taxpayer and transparent governance. The site makes a direct zero-based-budget commitment, calls for every dollar to produce measurable results, supports police funding, opposes current Missing Middle/OCP density rules, advocates stronger community input and records her attempt to seek closure of The Harbour supervised-consumption site. Its statement that City reserves fell from about $300 million to $160 million is a campaign claim that was not independently reconciled in this pass and should not be quoted as a verified City statistic without checking reserve definitions. The current Facebook page is linked directly by the campaign; X identifies itself as her only campaign account. No clearly attributable campaign Instagram, Bluesky, Threads, TikTok or YouTube account was located.

Sources. 2026 campaign site, priorities, voting record, City biography, 29 Apr re-election coverage, current social profiles, monthly newsletters, council records, Capital Daily and Times Colonist reporting.

Current-platform note. The explicit promise “I will zero-base the budget” materially strengthens the evidence for M65 and is more specific than the July record. The platform still lacks a defined review cycle, programme test, dollar savings target, residential tax glide path, debt and reserve ratios, Household Bill, permit benchmark and quarterly city-wide dashboard. Existing mark counts below are retained until the 11 Sep lock; the new zero-based-budget commitment should be incorporated in that formal crosswalk.

📋 Accountability. Opposed every budget and every Missing Middle vote — a consistent record. No published position on managed competition despite the strongest method rhetoric on council, which is the clearest gap between her framing and a mechanism.
