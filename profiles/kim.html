Profile. Susan Kim (김수잔) is a first-term Victoria councillor with a background in food security, community organizing, adult education, policy and operations. The City biography records work with both provincial and federal governments and Island Health, as well as involvement with the Civic Orchestra of Victoria, LifeCycles Project Society, the Victoria Korean Women’s Association and union locals. Kim’s 2022 profile described a trilingual Korean-Canadian settler, renter and then-director of operations at Fernwood NRG. First elected with 14,005 votes—the second-highest council total in 2022—Kim has emphasized renter protections, non-market housing, transit, climate action, harm reduction and access to public space. For voters, the 2026 campaign adds a significant governance fact: Kim is no longer running only as an independent but with the openly democratic-socialist Victoria For All organization, while also seeking a directly elected CRD position.

2026 campaign and social verification. Victoria For All publicly endorsed Kim on 15 Aug 2026 for re-election and for CRD director, alongside council candidate Peter Rose Gibbs and school-board candidate Maiya Beech. The organization identifies itself as a “working class, democratic, socialist, electoral and community organization.” Its four campaign pillars are housing, care, climate and community. Published commitments include doubling City spending on affordable public, non-profit and co-operative housing while protecting existing affordable rentals; creating a 24-hour mental-health crisis response; expanding outreach and harm-reduction access; expanding free transit; investing in heat pumps, green roofs and urban trees; and creating more gathering places and arts, recreation and cultural events. The personal susankim.ca site did not load, so the slate site is the current platform source of record. Kim’s Facebook and Instagram remain online; Instagram’s bio is current but general rather than explicitly electoral. A current-post chronology for X was not reliably available. The slate maintains active Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Threads and Bluesky identities and links them directly from its website.

📋 Contested item on the public record, added July 31 2026 because its absence from this page was a completeness failure. In November 2023 her name appeared on an archived open letter, first signed by former Ontario MPP Sarah Jama, which called for a Gaza ceasefire and cast doubt on sexual-violence accusations arising from the October 7 Hamas attack. Per CBC News, a petition "with over 8,500 signatures as of Monday evening" sought "her official censure… a ban on her wearing the Palestinian keffiyeh scarf in council and her removal from all official board appointments." BC Opposition leader Kevin Falcon called for her resignation and Municipal Affairs Minister Anne Kang said she should apologise. Two qualifications belong with it: CBC reported it could not independently verify that she signed the letter, and Jama later stated she was not the author and asked to be removed. This is recorded as a contested item, not scored against any measure. It is not a municipal policy position and does not enter the four-value scale. It is here because a voter judging her is entitled to find it on the page that claims to be the complete record.
