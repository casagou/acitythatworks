Profile

Stephen Hammond

Council candidate · Victoria 2026

Decision 14—

Campaign. votestephenhammond.ca

Evidence vintage (summary). votestephenhammond.ca located July 31 2026 (this page previously recorded none) + July 6 launch coverage + authored motions + 4 yrs record

Web & socials. votestephenhammond.ca · IG · FB

Sources. Campaign site votestephenhammond.ca located July 31 2026 — this page previously recorded no 2026 site, which was wrong and understated his evidence base. Launch coverage: Victoria News, July 6 2026. Council record, authored motions, CBC, Times Colonist. City biography: victoria.ca. BA (Manitoba), JD (Osgoode Hall), non-practising member of the Law Society of BC; professional speaker and author. Launch framing: "a safer downtown, safer parks and public spaces, more sensible spending, and housing decisions that are practical, not ideological," and a stated aim of bringing "practical judgement back to the council table." In Victoria since 2005. Boards: Western Institute for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing, Tenant Resource and Advisory Centre, Our Place Society, Gorge View Housing Society. Campaign manager Jack Boomer.

✅ Aligned (6) — three of them corrected onto this page July 30. M33: he authored the daytime sheltering ban, July 2024, passed 8-1. M28: his FOI work surfaced that roughly 24% of all 2022 police calls came from 19 identified addresses. M33b: he drafted the Sept 2023 illicit-drug-use 30m buffer-zone bylaw, shelved on the provincial pivot. These three were sourced in this document's own text and had no table to live in, so the scorecard was not counting them. That was the largest single unfairness on this page and it ran against him. Plus capacity-first sequencing (M11), NO on every annual tax increase (M65/M66), and the downloading frame, "people are expecting us to do things in which the province and the feds have the ability to spend and go into debt for" (M70b).

🟢 Close (2). FOI-driven publication of enforcement data as a real reporting mechanism, narrower than a quarterly dashboard (M67). The security-cost compensation motion, July 2023, defeated 6-2, proposing City help funding private security for businesses disproportionately exposed to crime spillover, absorbed as M28b (M71).

🟡 Partial (5). "Enforce existing bylaws consistently" is a direct match to M33 and not to M27's hours, which he has no position on. CSWB support without a unified-command structure (M26). NO on the VicPD ask cut (M29). General "reduce red tape" with no benchmark mechanism (M7). Voting NO on every increase on a household-burden argument, without a debt rule or utility-inclusive figure (M66d). Council-conduct accountability advocacy, downgraded from ✅ in v1.8 because no statement naming an Integrity Commissioner was located (M79b).

❌ Opposed (1). NO on Missing Middle, Jan 2023 and Sept 2023 (M6).

📋 Accountability. Opposed every budget, which is a consistent record rather than a gap. No permit benchmark mechanism in four years despite a red-tape posture. ❌ on managed competition removed in v1.8 for having no source.

Guiding philosophy. "The job of council is not to run the city, but to ensure the city is run well."
