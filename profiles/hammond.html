Who they are

Stephen Hammond is a first-term Victoria councillor, lawyer by training, professional speaker, consultant and author focused on workplace harassment, discrimination, bullying and human rights. He holds a BA from the University of Manitoba and a JD from Osgoode Hall, was called to the bar in 1988 and remains a non-practising member of the Law Society of British Columbia. He has lived in Victoria since 2005 and has served on the boards of Our Place Society, Gorge View Society, the Tenant Resource and Advisory Centre and the Western Institute for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing, while volunteering as a Big Brother mentor. First elected in 2022, he has been one of council’s most consistent dissenting votes on budgets, land-use changes and public-safety questions. For voters, that gives him a clearly differentiated record; the test is whether opposition is paired with a complete, fiscally workable alternative.

Current-platform note. The new site gives voters a much clearer 2026 programme than the July snapshot. It commits to a spending review and reserve protection but does not specify a multi-year residential tax cap, savings target, debt ratios, protected reserve floor or programme-by-programme review method. Its housing and safety commitments are concrete in direction but do not include unit targets, permit standards, staffing, service hours or costs. Existing marks below are retained pending the 11 Sep lock; the new tenant-compensation and spending-review language should be included in that crosswalk.

FOI source located — and it exposes a figure error in the Program. The public source for the release behind M28 is Global News, 11 Aug 2023: he obtained VicPD call data by FOI and published it, saying "Unfortunately, this data shows what many Victoria residents, businesses, and visitors already know: a relatively small number of shelters and parks where people shelter create a disproportionate number of police calls for service." Reported figures: 19 shelter and encampment locations account for about 25% of all police calls; 9,363 shelter-and-park calls in 2022; more than 29,000 over 2020–22; Rock Bay Landing alone 3,337 over three years. Row 9 of his card is now a shown 📋 Record line, the seventh. Action on the Program, not on his card: M28 currently reads "about 24% of 2022 police calls from 19 addresses". The published report says about 25% of all police calls at 19 shelter and encampment locations, and 9,363 is the 2022 shelter-and-park subtotal, not the base. Correct M28's wording before that figure appears in voter-facing material — it is the kind of number that gets checked in public.
