Profile. Jeremy Caradonna is a first-term Victoria councillor and CRD director with a PhD in History from Johns Hopkins University. Before elected office, he worked as a tenured professor in history and environmental studies, a provincial policymaker in climate action and jobs, and the owner of a Rock Bay organic-food delivery business. He has lived in Fernwood since 2012, first as a renter and later as a homeowner, and lives there with his spouse and two teenage children. His current regional work includes the Victoria Regional Transit Commission and the Capital Region Housing Corporation, where he serves as vice-chair. For voters, his record combines housing-supply, climate, transit, arts and social-policy advocacy; because he topped the 2022 council poll with 14,238 votes and has held both City and CRD roles, his 2026 promises should be assessed against concrete first-term outcomes as well as stated intentions.

2026 campaign and social verification. electjeremy.ca is now a current, authorized re-election site with a biography, platform, achievements, endorsements, videos, donations and campaign sign-up. It reports more than 9,000 homes approved during the term, including more than 2,000 non-market homes and 4,000 purpose-built rentals, and foregrounds housing, community safety and wellbeing, climate, transportation, arts and recreation. These are campaign claims and approvals, not occupancy counts. The platform uses expandable sections whose complete text was not consistently exposed in the accessible page extract, so individual claims should continue to be checked before scoring. Facebook and Instagram are visibly updated for re-election; Instagram identifies him as a councillor and CRD director running again. The X account is the published 2022 handle, but a reliable current-post chronology was not available. No separate TikTok, Threads or Bluesky campaign account was clearly linked from the accessible site text.

Sources. 2026 campaign site, campaign biography, City biography, CRD/Transit Commission records, December 2025 re-election coverage, and council records. The candidate’s new website is the principal source for 2026 promises; its achievement totals remain campaign-attributed until reconciled with City approvals and occupancy data.

Current-platform note. The campaign’s stated objective is to make Victoria “the safest, most prosperous, and sustainable small city in the world.” It presents the next term as an extension of the first: accelerate diverse housing, implement the Community Safety and Wellbeing Plan, continue climate and transportation work, complete the Crystal Pool replacement and protect cultural infrastructure. No new exact ACTW-style permit benchmark, tax glide path, debt rule, city-wide quarterly dashboard or managed-competition mechanism was located in the accessible platform text. The existing marks below are therefore retained pending the 11 Sep lock rather than increased from broad campaign framing.

📋 Accountability. Voted for every budget in the term including the 7.28% aggregate. Called Coun. Hammond a "liar" over the pool and apologised. No permit benchmarks, no dashboard, no ZBB position in four years. His ❌ on managed competition was removed in v1.8 for having no source — he has never stated a position on competitive testing.
