Framework/Candidate Profiles/Jeremy Caradonna
Profile

Jeremy Caradonna

Council candidate · Victoria 2026

Decision 14—

Campaign. electjeremy.ca

Evidence vintage (summary). No 2026 platform site located. Press release + outlet coverage + 4 yrs record

Web & socials. electjeremy.ca · IG

Sources. No dedicated 2026 campaign site located; announcing through press release and outlet coverage (Victoria News Dec 21 2025, Victoria Buzz Dec 19 2025). Council record, authored motions, LinkedIn. Topped the 2022 poll with 14,238 votes.

✅ Aligned (3). Co-author of the May 2026 cooling bylaw motion, 26°C, New Westminster precedent (M9/M64). Climate leadership (M58). Caledonia Place regional motion, "the city for too long has done it all" (M70b).

🟢 Close (3), two of them new July 30. Council adopted the city's first family housing policy and approved nearly 9,000 homes including non-market and rental — a family housing policy is a composition instrument and this page previously recorded him as having none (M6). He also drafted the Sept 2023 Missing Middle overhaul mechanics: dropped bonus-density costs, raised heights, livable basements plus three above-ground storeys. He advanced a new CRD transportation service as CRD vice-chair — an act, not the director's seat that banned inference 5 excludes (M70c, M23b).

🟡 Partial (5). Pro-transit and pro-bike with no adaptive-signal commitment (M19). Sponsored the VicPD 2026 ask cut, 4 to 2 patrol officers, "the ability of Victoria taxpayers to pay for police services is stretched to the absolute max" — fiscal restraint from the progressive side, not regional consolidation (M29). Voted YES on the 2026 budget (M65/M66). Crystal Pool YES, no general threshold (M77). "A miniature London or Paris rather than a miniature Vancouver or Manhattan" as an aesthetic frame (M43).

📋 Accountability. Voted for every budget in the term including the 7.28% aggregate. Called Coun. Hammond a "liar" over the pool and apologised. No permit benchmarks, no dashboard, no ZBB position in four years. His ❌ on managed competition was removed in v1.8 for having no source — he has never stated a position on competitive testing.

⚪ Still. Permits, managed competition, the dashboard, cleanliness standards, families and schools, accessibility, streetlighting, the Integrity Commissioner, the Household Bill, neighbourhoods and turnout.
