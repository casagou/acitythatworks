Profile. Melissa Cseszko (pronounced “Chess-ko”) is a lifelong Victoria resident, downtown resident and small-business owner. She has operated Mel Lingerie on Fort Street for five years and entered civic politics after repeated break-ins and first-hand experience with street disorder, business costs and declining confidence downtown. Her campaign emphasizes practical rather than ideological leadership, with public safety, measurable performance, heritage and local-business vitality at its centre. For voters, her direct experience is highly relevant to the downtown economy; the outstanding question is how her diagnosis would translate into municipal staffing, service standards, budgets and treatment or housing partnerships.

2026 campaign and social verification. melissacseszko.ca is a current, authorized council campaign with donation, volunteer, lawn-sign and contact functions. Its programme has three pillars: rebuild safety, through predictable rules and consistent standards; restore accountability, through oversight, transparency, performance review and adjustment of programmes that do not deliver; and revive Victoria, protecting heritage, historic buildings, local businesses and Victoria’s distinctive character. The newest dated website update located is 15 May. The website does not link Facebook, Instagram, X, Bluesky, Threads, TikTok or YouTube, and searches returned ambiguous or unrelated accounts; no dedicated current campaign social channel should be attributed without direct confirmation.

Sources. Campaign site, 12 May launch coverage, CFAX interview and earlier CHEK reporting. Earlier reporting framed a potential mayoral run, but the candidate’s current authorized site and May announcement confirm City Council as the office sought.

Current-platform note. The refreshed site is more than a slogan page but is not yet a full operating plan. It names no police or bylaw complement, non-police response model, cleanliness standard, downtown-vacancy target, tax limit, programme-review method, budget saving, heritage tool or reporting cadence. Existing marks below remain a pre-lock assessment; re-score if a detailed release appears before or after the 11 Sep nomination close.
