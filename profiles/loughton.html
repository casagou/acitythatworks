Krista Loughton

Council candidate · Victoria 2026

Web, campaign & socials (checked 1 Sep 2026). Campaign site · Instagram · Threads · X · YouTube · Facebook—2026 campaign · Facebook—councillor page · City biography

Profile. Krista Loughton is a first-term Victoria councillor, award-winning documentary filmmaker and business owner whose work has centred on poverty, trauma, homelessness and addiction. Her feature documentary Us and Them has screened across North America, including on Parliament Hill and at the BC Legislature. She holds a specialist BA in geography and, during the current term, has served on heritage and theatre bodies and as president of the Canadian Capital Cities Organization executive. She lives in Fernwood. For voters, her strongest claim is direct subject-matter engagement on homelessness and supportive housing; the corresponding accountability question is whether City-led interventions have produced measurable transitions indoors and whether a simultaneous CRD bid would improve regional cost- and service-sharing.

2026 campaign and social verification. kristaloughton.ca is now a current, authorized campaign site for both council and CRD director, with volunteer, donation, lawn-sign and survey functions. Its stated priorities are downtown conditions, affordability and regionalizing homelessness services so Victoria does not continue carrying the regional load alone. The June launch adds specific support for finding indoor spaces for roughly 200 unsheltered residents, expanding federal and provincial investment, making better use of existing housing and facilities, and supporting Positive Flow—rent supplements and light supports that help supportive-housing residents move into independent housing, freeing supportive and shelter spaces. The site still says community feedback will inform the platform, so this is not yet a fully costed programme. Instagram and Threads visibly carry the 2026 campaign launch and priorities. X, YouTube and two Facebook identities are linked from the campaign or prior public record, but reliable current-post chronologies were not available for all of them.

Sources. 2026 campaign site, 3 Jun launch coverage, City biography, campaign-linked social profiles, 2022 candidate material and council records. The earlier volunteer-list misspelling “Laughton” is superseded by direct campaign confirmation.

Current-platform note. Positive Flow, regional service coordination and a CRD candidacy are material new context and strengthen the evidence around M11/M26/M70b. The campaign has not yet published costs, annual transition targets, available-unit counts, regional contribution formulas or an accountability dashboard. Existing marks below are retained until the 11 Sep lock rather than changed from an unfinished platform.

📋 Accountability. YES on every budget despite stated reservations. No permit benchmarks, no dashboard. ❌ on managed competition removed in v1.8 for having no source.

Idea the framework lacks. A dedicated Island Health outreach worker embedded with the City's relocation team — the operational mechanism that makes relocation work at the individual level.
