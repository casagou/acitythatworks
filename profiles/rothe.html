Who they are

Karen Rothe is a lifelong Victorian, Vic West resident and downtown worker with a public-service career focused on planning and prevention, municipal affairs and emergency management. She served for 22 years as the property-management coordinator for her strata and has volunteered at City events. Professionally, she has worked on the 2017 Williams Lake wildfire evacuation, sustained provincial funding for BC Search and Rescue and the provincial team responsible for Metro Vancouver’s regional growth strategy. For voters, that offers unusually relevant experience in emergency coordination, regional planning and long-horizon infrastructure decisions; the corresponding test is whether a general stewardship platform becomes a specific City budget and delivery programme.

Current-platform note. The campaign identifies priorities and a governing approach but does not publish a fiscal baseline, tax path, capital schedule, permit standard, emergency-preparedness inventory, transit commitment, public-safety staffing plan or recurring performance dashboard. Rothe’s emergency-management and regional-planning experience is relevant background, not evidence of an unspoken policy. As of 2 Sep 2026 she is declared but not nominated — the City lists accepted nominations for Alto and Atkinson (Mayor) and Gardiner (Council). Nominations close 11 Sep at 4 PM, and her column is provisional until then.
