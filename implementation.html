The ownership rule

Every measure has one named, accountable owner, published in a mandate letter within the first 30 days (M79c). A measure with two owners has none.

Where delivery depends on a body council does not control — the police board, the transit commission, the CRD — the letter names the City's position and its contribution, not an outcome the City cannot lawfully order. That distinction is the spine of the Legal Defensibility Annex.

The first 100 days

◆ Stated up front

Everything below runs inside lines already costed in the Savings & Revenue Analysis. There is no new money on this list, and there are no savings claims beyond the Year-1 realization band already published ($1.4–2.7M full-year, Part A6) — most of which lands after day 100. That is stated up front because a 100-day list that quietly promises steady-state results is the kind of list this framework exists to replace.

Days 1–30

Set the operating system

  1. Lean-baseline budget instruction issued — the deferred additions from the council's 13.3% opening draft are not re-adopted by default, the first structural decision named in Part E.
  2. Attrition-only policy in force — departures reviewed before backfill (M68).
  3. Mandate letters published for every portfolio above (M79c).
  4. The material-change rule adopted — mid-year surprises are disclosed when they are known, not when convenient (M78b). Council remuneration deferral adopted (M79f).
  5. First dashboard cut published from data the City already holds — the baselines the KPI library locks (M67).
Days 31–60

Start the savings engine, and the visible work

  1. Managed-competition tender issued for waste and street cleaning, with the employee-protection clauses stated in the tender (M15).
  2. First zero-based review scoped — grants and contracts over $50K, with condition verification (M65, M9b extension).
  3. Extended bylaw hours phase-in begins toward 6 AM–10 PM, seven days; the Downtown Public Order Team stands up inside that line (M27, M26 — $1.0–1.5M/yr as booked).
  4. The 24-month suite amnesty and concierge open (M8b — years 1–2 add-back, funded from M15 savings as booked).
  5. Partnership letters go to BC Housing, Island Health and the Province on the exact asks in the Partnership Strategy. The gated measures (M26b, M28d) remain at $0 until a cost-share partner signs — the gate is the negotiating position.
Days 61–100

Build the instruments

  1. Pattern-book design competition launched inside the M7 platform envelope (M7b).
  2. Lobbyist-registry build starts (M79d); Integrity Commissioner shared-service negotiation opens (M79b).
  3. School District 61 standing-agreement talks begin (M13d).
  4. Sidewalk inventory and crossing-gap analysis tendered, sequenced with the LIDAR scan to reduce cost (M20b, M53c).
  5. The first quarterly variance report is published — the first receipt. If any of items 1–14 slipped, the report says so and says why.

◆ The rule that makes the cadence mean something

A miss is published with a variance note and a correction path, in the same release, at the same prominence as the wins. A framework that grades the current council on published outcomes accepts the same grading.
