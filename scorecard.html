How we grade

Actions first: if they held office and a sourced vote or decision contradicts a 2026 promise, the action counts.

Every candidate, every topic

Gen is the overall letter. The other columns are the same 55 topics grouped by area. A filled cell is a sourced mark — tap it to open that citation on the candidate’s profile. A dash is silence, not a fail. Sort, filter, or tick candidates to compare them.

How to read a row
  • BThe letter, in the Gen column only. Applied by hand from the published rules, never computed on this page, and only once a candidate has five answered measures. The letters in use are B, B−, C+, C, C− and D, or a dash where none has been ruled. The card mean and the number of answered measures are printed beside it, because an unweighted mean rewards a thin card.
  • 3/7A topic cell: how many of that topic's measures carry a sourced answer, out of how many the topic holds. No topic gets a letter or an average — that is a rule of the method, not an omission.
  • RRecord: a documented fact about a term in office. Shown, never scored, in either direction.
  • —Nothing located yet. A dash is unknown, not a fail, and not opposition.

Every filled cell is checkable. Select one and it opens the candidate's own profile at the sentence the mark rests on, with the source link and the date it was retrieved. The rules a mark has to meet are published further down this page, and the full two-question scale with its bands is on the evidence store. Corrections are free and dated: info@acitythatworks.ca.
