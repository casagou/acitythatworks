◆ Read this before you read the grades

A dash is not a bad grade. It means no public position on that topic was located — which is the most common result. Of 480 candidate-topic cells, 291 (61%) had no public position. Absence of evidence is not opposition, and it is not scored as one.

Grades reflect only the topics where a position was found. Each cell shows how many of its topics were sourced — hover or open a row to see. A candidate graded on 2 of 9 topics is not comparable to one graded on 9.

This is a policy comparison, not an endorsement. The framework endorses no candidate, and every candidate is free to adopt any measure in it. Corrections: info@acitythatworks.ca.
