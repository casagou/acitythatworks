How we grade

We score the same 55 topics for every candidate.

Actions first: if they held office and a sourced vote or decision contradicts a 2026 promise, the action counts.

Words next: a 2026 campaign sentence with a URL and date.

Silence last: no public answer is a dash, not a fail. Letter only at 5 scored answers.

Every candidate, every topic

Gen is the overall letter. The other columns are the same 55 topics grouped by area. A filled cell is a sourced mark — tap it to open that citation on the candidate’s profile. A dash is silence, not a fail. Sort, filter, or tick candidates to compare them.

How to read a row
  • BThe letter, in the Gen column only. Applied by hand from the published rules, never computed on this page, and only once a candidate has five answered measures. The five letters in use are B+, B, C, D and —.
  • 3/7A topic cell: how many of that topic's measures carry a sourced answer, out of how many the topic holds. No topic gets a letter or an average — that is a rule of the method, not an omission.
  • RRecord: a documented fact about a term in office. Shown, never scored, in either direction.
  • —Nothing located yet. A dash is unknown, not a fail, and not opposition.

Every filled cell is checkable. Select one and it opens the candidate's own profile at the sentence the mark rests on, with the source link and the date it was retrieved. The rules, the two questions behind every mark and the bands are published further down this page. Corrections are free and dated: info@acitythatworks.ca.
