Candidate Questionnaire

Every candidate for Mayor and Council in the City of Victoria gets the same 46 questions. The full set has been public since the day it was first sent, so anyone can see exactly what everyone else was asked.

Answers are published word for word on 1 October, in full, next to every other candidate's — with no grades, no scores and no ranking. Deadline for answers: Thursday 25 September, 5 p.m.

Are you a candidate?

Fill it in right here. Your answers save as you go, in this browser, so you can stop and come back. Nothing leaves your device until you send it yourself, at the end.

  • Skip anything you like. We write “no answer” and we do not count it against you.
  • Throughout, we are more interested in what you will actually do, and by when, than in what you are broadly in favour of.
  • Prefer paper, or want to send it as a file? Download the same questionnaire as a fillable PDF.

What happens to your answers

They are published word for word and in full on 1 October, unedited and without commentary, next to every other candidate's. No grades, no scores, no ranking.

  • Before anything is published we email your answers back to you for a 48-hour check, and we accept corrections at any time afterwards.
  • Where a candidate does not answer, we record what their published platform says, with a link and a date — and a dash where we could find nothing.
  • A City That Works is the author of a written municipal program published on this site. These questions are drawn from the subjects it covers. It is a standard we are open about, not a hidden one.

Reading rather than answering? This is the questionnaire itself, exactly as it was sent — the fields below are here for candidates, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Answers, when they arrive, appear on the candidate scorecard.

VIC-07

Camping in parks, and what the courts allow

Two court decisions, Adams in 2009 and Bamberger in 2022, say a city can't stop people sheltering in parks unless there is somewhere safe and available for them to go. The 2025 count found 1,749 people homeless in Greater Victoria, 318 of them sleeping outside. Three quarters said they had a reason to avoid shelters, most often that they didn't feel safe there.

CRD / Community Social Planning Council 2025 Point-in-Time Count, released September 2025

How would you apply the City's sheltering rules?

VIC-09

What Pandora should be in four years

Since 2023 the City has spent roughly $11 million on repairs and added bylaw presence in and around the 900-block of Pandora, on top of about $12 million a year on its homelessness response. Victoria holds about 89% of the region's shelter spaces and 83% of its supportive housing. BC Housing's Bridge Street Pathways opened in January 2026 with 34 spaces, prioritised for people on Pandora. The block still carries daily sheltering, open drug use, and the businesses and services that were there before.

City of Victoria; BC Housing; Globe and Mail

A · Four years from now, what should the 900-block of Pandora be?
