<callout icon="⚖️" color="gray_bg">
	**On accountability for sitting members.** Incumbents are assessed on two separate things and the page keeps them separate. **A stated position** is what they say they would do, scored ✅ 🟢 🟡 ❌. **A record** is what the council did or did not do, marked 📋 and never scored. An incumbent who never acted on a measure has not opposed it. But a record that contradicts a stated position, or a four-year gap on something within the City's own control, **is stated plainly in the accountability block and is the fair basis on which a voter judges an incumbent.** Challengers have no such block because they have no record, and that asymmetry is a fact about the office, not a thumb on the scale.

"Reimagine transportation" with no jurisdictional line, capital figure or sidewalk commitment (M20b, M23b). Climate Leadership Plan support with Climate Friendly Homes uptake unpublished (M58). Crystal Pool as a one-off referendum precedent, no general threshold (M77).
**📋 Accountability — what the council did, on the record.** Four consecutive years above inflation: 6.15%, 7.93%, 6.99%, and **7.28% aggregate in 2026, which is 9.34% residential and 4.78% business**, about **\$323 more on a median \$1,015,000 home**. The 2026 budget **opened at 13.3%** in November and roughly \$7M of the reduction came from one-time measures including a **cancelled \$2M debt-reduction payment** and a **\$2.8M parking-reserve draw** — an approach she described herself as not a long-term solution, which is the exact conduct M66c is written to prevent. Operating budget **\$300.0M adopted 2023 to \$394.1M adopted 2026, roughly 31%**. **Zero modern efficiency tools in four years**: no ZBB, no competitive benchmarking, no permit-processing benchmarks, no quarterly dashboard, no municipal AI standard. **MNP governance report not implemented** despite being on file. **Climate Friendly Homes uptake not published.** Missing Middle delivered **61 permits in 2024 against a 150 target**. CSWB Phase 1 funded by drawing **\$2.5M from Centennial Square, \$4M from the Financial Stability Reserve and \$1.5M from Royal Athletic Park**. **Dowler Place**: a confidential draft motion authored by her, to convert Dowler Place into a Pandora relocation site, was [disclosed by CHEK via an FOI in which the documents were accidentally attached, June 2025](https://cheknews.ca/exclusive-victoria-mayors-confidential-draft-reveals-plan-to-shift-pandora-problems-into-new-community-1260507/), with consultation skipped because "we knew what the comments would be from the public." She [issued a statement](https://cheknews.ca/victoria-mayor-responds-to-chek-story-on-draft-plan-for-dowler-place-1260738/): "I recognize that there has been confusion and frustration… residents haven't had a full or accurate picture of the project… I apologize for that confusion." **A related item belongs on the record and was missing from this page:** firefighter Josh Montgomery was suspended one day for an open letter to Premier Eby opposing the hub; Alto declined to apologise to him and [publicly told the Premier that local personnel matters were "not his bailiwick"](https://www.timescolonist.com/local-news/victoria-mayor-suggests-premier-stay-in-his-lane-in-firefighter-suspension-9363506) (*Times Colonist*, 2024). **She has stated no position against any of these measures. This is what a council did across four budgets, and it is the baseline any 2027 council starts from.**
**⚪ Still.** Permits and AI pre-screening, managed competition, the performance dashboard, accessibility and washrooms, streetlighting, GIS and AI governance, the Integrity Commissioner, the regional bill, the Household Bill.
**What would move it.** A published position on any of the five method measures. Four years of a mayoralty produce Close marks; the method gap is a record, and only a stated commitment closes it.

## New developments, June–July 2026
<callout icon="📌" color="orange_bg">

### 1. The police board is appealing the council's budget
The **Victoria and Esquimalt Police Board voted unanimously to appeal** the City's reduction of four new officers to two. Board member Elizabeth Cull stated that "four people are needed for a patrol." Under the *Police Act*, an unresolved municipal-police funding dispute goes to the **provincial director of police services**, who has historically sided with police boards. Source: [*Victoria News*](https://vicnews.com/2026/06/22/victoria-police-board-to-appeal-citys-2026-budget/)[ / ](https://vicnews.com/2026/06/22/victoria-police-board-to-appeal-citys-2026-budget/)[*Oak Bay News*](https://vicnews.com/2026/06/22/victoria-police-board-to-appeal-citys-2026-budget/)[, June 22 2026](https://vicnews.com/2026/06/22/victoria-police-board-to-appeal-citys-2026-budget/).
**Why it matters for these profiles.** **The 5-4 vote that four candidates on this page are accountable for may be overturned by a body none of them sits on.** It is a live demonstration of the jurisdictional limit the framework's city-hall page describes, and it should be raised with every candidate who has taken a position on the police budget in either direction.
### 2. The amalgamation question is confirmed on the October 17 ballot
Victoria and Saanich councils have agreed to place the same **non-binding** question on the ballot: **"Do you support Saanich and Victoria becoming one municipality?"** Source: [victoria.ca Elections](https://www.victoria.ca/city-government/elections).
**Why it matters.** M81's mechanism is live and on the ballot the same day these candidates are. **Coun. Coleman chairs the committee holding this file and has not declared.**
### 3. Full ballot scope, October 17 2026
Victoria electors will elect **one mayor, eight councillors, three CRD directors and SD61 school trustees**, plus the amalgamation question. Source: [victoria.ca](http://victoria.ca).
**Why it matters.** **Three CRD Director positions are on the ballot and no candidate on this page campaigns on CRD cost** — the M70c gap is not theoretical, it is a full column of ⚪ on a ballot-relevant \$1.07B question. **SD61 trustees are elected the same day, which is the practical opening for M13d.**
