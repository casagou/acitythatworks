**Profile.** Melissa Cseszko (pronounced “Chess-ko”) is a lifelong Victoria resident, downtown resident and small-business owner. She has operated Mel Lingerie on Fort Street for five years and entered civic politics after repeated break-ins and first-hand experience with street disorder, business costs and declining confidence downtown. Her campaign emphasizes practical rather than ideological leadership, with public safety, measurable performance, heritage and local-business vitality at its centre. For voters, her direct experience is highly relevant to the downtown economy; the outstanding question is how her diagnosis would translate into municipal staffing, service standards, budgets and treatment or housing partnerships.
**2026 campaign and social verification.** [melissacseszko.ca](https://www.melissacseszko.ca/) is a current, authorized council campaign with donation, volunteer, lawn-sign and contact functions. Its programme has three pillars: **rebuild safety**, through predictable rules and consistent standards; **restore accountability**, through oversight, transparency, performance review and adjustment of programmes that do not deliver; and **revive Victoria**, protecting heritage, historic buildings, local businesses and Victoria’s distinctive character.
