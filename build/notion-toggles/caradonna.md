**Profile.** Jeremy Caradonna is a first-term Victoria councillor and CRD director with a PhD in History from Johns Hopkins University. Before elected office, he worked as a tenured professor in history and environmental studies, a provincial policymaker in climate action and jobs, and the owner of a Rock Bay organic-food delivery business. He has lived in Fernwood since 2012, first as a renter and later as a homeowner, and lives there with his spouse and two teenage children. His current regional work includes the Victoria Regional Transit Commission and the Capital Region Housing Corporation, where he serves as vice-chair. For voters, his record combines housing-supply, climate, transit, arts and social-policy advocacy; because he topped the 2022 council poll with 14,238 votes and has held both City and CRD roles, his 2026 promises should be assessed against concrete first-term outcomes as well as stated intentions.
