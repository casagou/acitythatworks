**Profile.** Jerry Garcia is a retired professional engineer, executive, entrepreneur and community volunteer with more than four decades of experience leading organizations and complex projects. His campaign biography describes work in Toronto, Montreal, Tokyo, Miami and elsewhere with global companies, governments, health-care organizations and small businesses, as well as teaching entrepreneurs. Garcia and his spouse chose downtown Victoria as their home eight years ago. For voters, his strongest case is engineering and executive delivery experience; the key scrutiny points are whether his ambitious performance targets use verifiable baselines and whether City and CRD authorities can deliver them.
**2026 campaign and social verification.** [jerryforvictoria.ca](https://jerryforvictoria.ca/) is a current campaign site for both Victoria City Council and CRD director, with a biography, articles, volunteer and donation functions and five “transformational changes”: grow the economy; restore safe public spaces; end chronic homelessness through housing, treatment and recovery; build complete neighbourhoods; and deliver faster, more transparent government. It commits to cutting permit times by at least 50%, reducing homelessness by 85%, publishing performance dashboards, using evidence-based budgeting, improving lighting and cleaning, supporting visible patrols and community safety teams, coordinating treatment and supportive housing, attracting investment and using smart-city technology. The site labels itself “Jerry 1.0” and says a second version is coming, so details may still change. Facebook and Instagram are linked directly and are the verified campaign channels; no other official social network was linked.
