**Profile.** Shona Dion is a labour activist, community builder, photographer and working parent who moved to Victoria with her family in 2013. She uses she/her pronouns and describes 28 years of union membership. Her community and governance experience includes the North Jubilee Neighbourhood Association, service as a Victoria Labour Council table officer and recording secretary, work as a job steward and executive councillor, and fiduciary responsibility as a benefit-plan trustee. She also volunteers photography for social-justice, inclusion and environmental organizations. For voters, Dion offers deep labour and grassroots governance experience; the key question is how her community-agency approach translates into City-controlled programmes, costs and measurable outcomes.
