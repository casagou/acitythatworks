**Profile.** Karen Rothe is a lifelong Victorian, Vic West resident and downtown worker with a public-service career focused on planning and prevention, municipal affairs and emergency management. She served for 22 years as the property-management coordinator for her strata and has volunteered at City events. Professionally, she has worked on the 2017 Williams Lake wildfire evacuation, sustained provincial funding for BC Search and Rescue and the provincial team responsible for Metro Vancouver’s regional growth strategy. For voters, that offers unusually relevant experience in emergency coordination, regional planning and long-horizon infrastructure decisions; the corresponding test is whether a general stewardship platform becomes a specific City budget and delivery programme.
