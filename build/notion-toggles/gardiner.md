**Profile.** Marg Gardiner is a first-term Victoria councillor with a background in chemistry, public management, regulatory enforcement, programme management and data analysis. She holds a BSc in chemistry and an MBA in public management/administration, has lived in Victoria for more than two decades and served for 16 years on the James Bay Neighbourhood Association board before election. Her community work has covered public safety, land use, accessibility, transportation, parks, economic development and air quality. On council she has been one of the most consistent fiscal- and public-safety dissenters, opposing each annual tax increase and the Missing Middle policy. For voters, her campaign offers the clearest method-based alternative on budgeting and neighbourhood consultation, while her record on policing, supervised consumption and land use makes the substantive trade-offs unusually explicit.
**2026 campaign and social verification.** [marggardiner.ca](https://marggardiner.ca/) is now a current, authorized campaign site for City Council and CRD director with biography, priorities, voting record, endorsements, donations, volunteering, news, voter survey and campaign email sign-up.
