**Profile.** Martin Girard is a full-time community advocate and activist whose public work has focused on homelessness, civil liberties, drug policy, human rights, environmental issues and accountability at Victoria City Hall. He describes five years of personal homelessness, three years of frontline volunteering, current residence in supportive housing and experience responding to overdoses with naloxone. Campaign material also describes an honours science education. He has appeared before council and in local reporting on encampment enforcement, SOGI, civil rights and transparency. For voters, Girard offers the field’s strongest lived-experience challenge to enforcement-led homelessness policy; his most consequential proposals also require close scrutiny for municipal authority, legal risk and implementation capacity.
