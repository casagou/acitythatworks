An Oak Bay Secondary graduate, she has worked as a senior government adviser and has campaign experience on regional cost-sharing for homelessness, Access BC’s successful free-contraception initiative and the Crystal Pool referendum. Her campaign also highlights experience in project management and public advocacy.
