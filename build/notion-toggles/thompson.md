**Profile.** Dave Thompson is a first-term Victoria councillor and CRD director, lawyer by training, small-business owner and governance and public-policy consultant. He has practised law in Ontario and British Columbia, worked for governments and non-profits, and co-founded a firm serving Indigenous communities across Canada. Before council he held several roles with the Fairfield Gonzales Community Association, including director, treasurer, vice-president, land-use committee member and neighbourhood-improvement chair. He has lived in four Victoria neighbourhoods as both renter and owner and lives in the city with his family. First elected in 2022 with 12,271 votes—the fifth-highest council total—he presents himself as an evidence-oriented progressive focused on housing, climate, mobility and social supports. Voters can judge that record alongside his support for every term budget and his concurrent bid for a CRD seat.
