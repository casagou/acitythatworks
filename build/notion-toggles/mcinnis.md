**Profile.** Arthur McInnis is a James Bay resident, law professor, former international construction and projects lawyer and former tribunal member with more than 30 years of experience examining government, infrastructure, finance and contractual decisions. He has taught law in Canada and abroad and describes four years of close analysis of Victoria planning and governance decisions before entering the race. For voters, his professional strength is institutional accountability: he offers the field’s most detailed legal and procedural programme, although many proposals would still require staff, statutory and financial feasibility review.
