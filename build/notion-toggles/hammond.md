**Profile.** Stephen Hammond is a first-term Victoria councillor, lawyer by training, professional speaker, consultant and author focused on workplace harassment, discrimination, bullying and human rights. He holds a BA from the University of Manitoba and a JD from Osgoode Hall, was called to the bar in 1988 and remains a non-practising member of the Law Society of British Columbia. He has lived in Victoria since 2005 and has served on the boards of Our Place Society, Gorge View Society, the Tenant Resource and Advisory Centre and the Western Institute for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing, while volunteering as a Big Brother mentor. First elected in 2022, he has been one of council’s most consistent dissenting votes on budgets, land-use changes and public-safety questions. For voters, that gives him a clearly differentiated record; the test is whether opposition is paired with a complete, fiscally workable alternative.
